# Public repository privacy policy

This source tree is intended to be portable and safe to share. Published paper
authorship, citations, methods, datasets, and scientific results are explicitly
in scope and should be preserved.

Do not commit:

- personal usernames, email addresses, or contact handles that are not part of
  a publication or licensing requirement;
- absolute home-directory paths, hostnames, cluster names, mount points, or
  storage-capacity details;
- private repository names, cloud project identifiers, scheduler records,
  credentials, tokens, or access instructions;
- raw participant data or participant-private metadata.

Use logical artifact IDs in committed manifests. Supply machine-specific paths
through environment variables or ignored local configuration.
